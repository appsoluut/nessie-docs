#### Skeleton loader as a circle

Example showing a circle that could be interpreted as a user avatar.

![Skeleton loader as a circle preview](./images/nl.ns.nessie.components.loader.samples.NesSkeleton_Circle_Sample.png)

```kotlin
NesSkeleton(
    shape = NesSkeletonShape.Circle,
    contentDescription = "User avatar"
)
```
